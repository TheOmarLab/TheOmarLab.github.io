We are excited to share our new preprint, *"THR-6E: A Six-Gene Cell-of-Origin Signature Stratifies Risk and Predicts Systemic Therapy Response in ER+/HER2− Breast Cancer."*

Building on our earlier work on the [triple hormone receptor (THR) signature](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13058-024-01876-9), THR-6E is a six-gene cell-of-origin signature that stratifies risk and predicts response to systemic therapy in patients with ER+/HER2− breast cancer. This work represents a step toward more precise risk assessment and treatment selection for this patient population.
