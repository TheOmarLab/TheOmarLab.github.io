We are excited to share our new preprint, *"THR-6E: A Six-Gene Cell-of-Origin Signature Stratifies Risk and Predicts Systemic Therapy Response in ER+/HER2− Breast Cancer,"* now available on [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.31.26345244v1).

Building on our earlier work on the [triple hormone receptor (THR) signature](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13058-024-01876-9), THR-6E is a compact six-gene cell-of-origin signature (KIF4A, KIF2C, CDC20, FAM64A, TPX2, LMNB2) that robustly stratifies relapse-free survival and predicts response to both endocrine therapy and chemotherapy in ER+/HER2− breast cancer, including in lymph node-positive disease. The signature was validated across >7,000 breast cancer cases from multiple independent cohorts, including the I-SPY2 adaptive clinical trial.
